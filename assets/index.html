How to play

Pause, sound, this panel

On a phone, swipe on the maze to steer. Keep your finger down and keep swiping.

Light every star

Walk over a star to light it, and it stays lit. Light them all to clear the sky. Light many without a pause to build a streak worth up to ×5.

Creatures are slower among lit stars, so your own trail is the safest place to run.

Flares

A flare shoots light down every corridor you can see. It lights the stars there and dazes every creature in its path, so you can walk straight past. Daze several at once for more points. Matches give you another flare.

In the clouds

Winds (white arrows) speed you along or hold you back. Doors come in pairs, and only you can use them. A passing paper crane is worth catching. Every 25,000 points is an extra spark.
